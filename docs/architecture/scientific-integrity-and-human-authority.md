# Scientific Integrity and Human Authority

## Constitutional rule
CBAI structures claims, evidence, methods, contradictions, uncertainties, and options.
CBAI never decides winners, personal superiority, permanent truth, or political outcomes.

## Roles
Permissions in `lib/scientific-deliberation/roles.ts`:
- Observer cannot approve
- Moderator cannot auto-become final scientific authority
- Institution impersonation and silent hide of counter-evidence forbidden
- External messages never automatic

## Human decision
Rooms close from `awaiting_human_decision` only with recorded human decision.
Living Synthesis: `finalTruthForbidden: true`; `humanApprovalStatus` distinct.

## Policy Evidence Deliberation
Never automates governmental/political decisions; Debate-to-Work notes reinforce this.
