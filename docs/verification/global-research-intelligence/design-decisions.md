# Global Research Intelligence — design decisions

## Purpose

Global Research Intelligence turns research material into decision-grade context. It
connects questions, methods, institutions, publications, limitations, counter-evidence,
unknowns, and possible next actions without presenting AI output as a final conclusion.

## Operating layout

The route follows the shared CBAI Intelligence OS hierarchy:

1. Active research question or selected domain.
2. Evidence coverage and provenance state.
3. Unknowns, limitations, and contradictions.
4. Methods, institutions, and related research paths.
5. Contextual Algorithm, Cybernetics, and AI support.
6. A human checkpoint before findings become a plan, report, outreach, or decision.

Secondary ecosystem, graph, and principles panels remain progressively disclosed so the
primary research task is not buried under a card catalogue.

## Intelligence roles

- **Human:** defines the purpose, assesses scientific relevance, and approves the next action.
- **Algorithm:** orders intake, validation, comparison, and review steps.
- **Cybernetics:** monitors feedback, measurement changes, and deviations when live inputs exist.
- **AI:** reads material, connects sources, detects contradictions, and develops explainable scenarios.

AI does not claim experimental validation, operate equipment, or approve scientific conclusions.

## Evidence and honesty rules

- Claims remain connected to traceable sources.
- Registry and catalogue relationships are not presented as verified scientific relationships.
- Missing measurements remain unknown and are never shown as zero.
- Opportunities must expose expiry and source status.
- Matching is deterministic and presents options, never an unsupported “best” result.
- A virtual laboratory is an analysis environment, not a claim that a physical experiment occurred.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop prioritizes the research canvas and keeps status context compact. Mobile preserves
the question, evidence status, unknowns, and human checkpoint before secondary graph or
ecosystem panels. Voice Operator remains available without obscuring primary controls.
