# University Intelligence — design decisions

## Purpose

University Intelligence connects institutions, research capabilities, laboratories,
publications, collaboration opportunities, and evidence to an active human problem.
It does not rank institutions or create an institutional recommendation without
traceable evidence.

## Operating layout

The page uses the shared CBAI Intelligence OS hierarchy:

1. Current institution and intended next step.
2. Evidence status and explicitly missing information.
3. Directory and filters in the navigator.
4. The selected university workspace in the primary canvas.
5. A compact context rail for source freshness and linked work.
6. A human checkpoint before outreach, presentation, or another consequential action.

The shared status rail keeps context, evidence, unknowns, and the human boundary visible
without duplicating the route's functional panels.

## Human, Algorithm, Cybernetics, and AI roles

- **Human:** selects the institution, evaluates evidence, and approves consequential actions.
- **Algorithm:** structures navigation, comparison, and the next-step sequence.
- **Cybernetics:** surfaces feedback, changes, and monitoring needs when a live signal exists.
- **AI:** connects sources, exposes gaps and contradictions, and explains possible paths.

No helper may silently create a decision, claim a verified partnership, or send a
presentation.

## Evidence and honesty rules

- Official-source facts and CBAI analysis remain visually and semantically distinct.
- Missing values are shown as unknown, never converted to zero.
- Scientific counts are not inferred from layout or catalog structure.
- University marks use a neutral monogram unless an authorized official asset exists.
- Opportunity and collaboration cards must not imply a live opening without a verified source.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses navigator, primary canvas, and context rail. Tablet and mobile preserve the
primary canvas, collapse filters behind an explicit control, and move secondary context
below the main work. Voice Operator remains available without covering the human checkpoint.
