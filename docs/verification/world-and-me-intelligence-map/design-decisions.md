# World and Me Intelligence Map — Design Decisions

1. Preserve the current graph vocabulary, projections, focus modes, and relationship model.
2. Keep the graph as an evidence navigation surface, not a decorative network.
3. Distinguish local registry/catalog relationships from verified live sources.
4. Expose context, evidence state, known unknowns, and human authority before advanced controls.
5. Require consent for “My World” and explicit confirmation before creating connected work.
6. Keep a structured list fallback for accessibility and low-capability devices.
7. Voice commands may navigate or draft a suggestion; they may not auto-send or auto-decide.
